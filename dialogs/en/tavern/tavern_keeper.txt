# He is only in the tavern before his death (TavernKeeper spot: HideIf = "tavern_keeper_dead").
# His death itself is yours to script: somewhere, "$set tavern_keeper_dead" starts the second act.
# A pint cost a stuiver or so (20 stuivers to the gulden); he charges the harbour two.
@show tavern_keeper
@if keeper_at_counter
  Tavern Keeper: Another pint, Harbourmaster? Same price as the first.
@else
  Tavern Keeper: Harbourmaster! Can I get you anything to drink?
  * A pint would be welcome.  @leave
    Tavern Keeper: Two stuivers, then. That's the harbour price.
    @if stuivers >= 2
      * Pay two stuivers.  @leave
        $add stuivers -2
        Tavern Keeper: Coming right up.
        @hide tavern_keeper
        $set keeper_at_counter
        $set cat_on_counter
        The Tavern Keeper goes behind the counter. The cat jumps off the table and pads after him.
        He pours you a pint. It is warm and flat, but it is beer.
        $journal done get_drink
      * On second thought, no.  @leave
        Tavern Keeper: Suit yourself.
    @else
      You pat your pockets. Not a single coin.
      Tavern Keeper: No coin, no beer. Not even for the harbour.
      $journal get_drink
  * Not now, thanks.  @leave
    Tavern Keeper: The barrel isn't going anywhere.
